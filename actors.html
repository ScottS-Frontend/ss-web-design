Why It Matters

Most self-submissions still get sent as a scatter of links — one for the reel, one for the resume, one for socials. Half of them break or get ignored. A single site puts your work in front of someone the moment they're deciding whether to keep scrolling or bring you in. It's a small thing that makes you look like you take the work seriously — because you do.

Common Questions

Do I need to buy a domain?

Yes — a domain (yourname.com) runs about $12–20/year. I'll help you register it, or set things up on one you already own.

What if I need more than one page?

This package is priced for a single-page site. If you want a full multi-page site with a blog, press section, or booking form, that falls under the Brochure or Business tiers instead — happy to talk through it.

Can I update it myself later?

Small text or link changes can be handled for you on request. If you want ongoing self-service updates or hosting handled entirely, ask about the Maintenance plan.

How fast is "5 business days," really?

That's typical once I have your materials in hand. If you're up against an audition deadline, just say so — faster turnaround may be possible.
